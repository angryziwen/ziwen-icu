# Ziwen Voices

> There is no Anran here. Only Ziwen.
>
> [中文：子文心声](<../子文心声/index.md>)

Ziwen Voices publishes first-person texts by BYD employees ("Ziwen"). No rewriting, no commentary, no speaking for anyone.

"Ziwen" is the self-deprecating name BYD employees use, taken from an internal comic. For background, see [The Origin of "Ziwen"](<../The Origin of Ziwen - A BYD Internal Comic and the Culture of Self-Deprecation.md>).

## Submissions

You don't have to represent anyone. Just tell us what happened to you.

- You can use your real name or a pseudonym. You can tell us about one incident or many years.
- Before publication, we will send you the passages that concern you for confirmation.
- After publication, if you change your mind, we can revise or withdraw it.

The submission channel is being prepared. For now, please contact the author through the site repository; **please do not put sensitive information in a public issue**.

## Content note

This column deals with workplace pressure, labour disputes, depression and suicide. Individual pieces carry their own content notes. If you or someone you know is in crisis, please contact a professional helpline first: China's national psychological assistance hotline **12356**, the Beijing Suicide Research and Prevention Center on **010-82951332**, or emergency services on **110** / **120**. Outside China, contact your local emergency number.
